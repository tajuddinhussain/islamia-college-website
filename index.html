About the College

ESTD. 1973

Islamia Arts & Science Degree & PG College is a minority educational institution located in Warangal, Telangana, India. The college is affiliated with Kakatiya University and managed by Islamia Education Society, Warangal.

The institution is dedicated to providing quality higher education, promoting academic excellence, and supporting the intellectual, personal, and professional development of students.

1973

College History

Established as a Degree College in 1973

With the continuous and dedicated efforts of its administration, the institution progressed steadily and was upgraded to become a Degree College in 1973. Since then, Islamia Arts & Science Degree & PG College has continued to serve students and contribute to the academic development of the Warangal region.

The institution was founded by Hazrath Syed Mohammed Hussain (Peace Be Upon Him).

The college remains committed to quality education, academic excellence, the overall development of students, and the educational values upon which the institution is founded.

Vision

To create an educational environment that promotes academic excellence, intellectual growth, ethical values, and lifelong learning, enabling students to become responsible and capable members of society.

Mission

  • To provide quality undergraduate and postgraduate education.
  • To encourage critical thinking, practical skills, and intellectual development.
  • To promote discipline, integrity, inclusiveness, and social responsibility.
  • To support students in developing their abilities and preparing for future academic and professional opportunities.

M.Com. (Computer Applications)

The college offers a postgraduate M.Com programme in Computer Applications, supported by dedicated computer laboratories.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Educational Qualification: Any Graduation from a recognized university, as specified in the TG CPGET Information Brochure.
  • Admission Process: Admission through the Telangana Common Post Graduate Entrance Test (TG CPGET), subject to the applicable eligibility conditions and counselling rules.
  • Course Duration: 2 years (4 semesters).

Important Note: Eligibility, qualifying marks, reservation benefits and admission requirements are subject to the latest official TG CPGET guidelines and university rules.

Official Admission Portal: https://cpget.ouadmissions.com/ (opens in a new tab)
